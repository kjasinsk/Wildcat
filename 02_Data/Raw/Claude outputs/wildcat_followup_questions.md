# Follow-Up Questions Provoked by the Wildcat Loan Analysis

## 1. Credit Metrics (Credit Score, DTI, Annual Income)

If a strong score and high DTI genuinely conflict, should Wildcat cap loan size or interest-rate pricing based on DTI even for high-score borrowers — and does the current underwriting process actually do that, or does score alone still drive approval?

## 2. Portfolio Committee Quarterly Review

Given that Business loans are a small share of loan count but a disproportionate share of dollar exposure, does Wildcat have a concentration limit on Business lending — and if not, what would trigger the committee to set one?

## 3. Delinquency vs. Default

If delinquency is a leading indicator, what's Wildcat's actual cure rate — what share of Delinquent loans return to Current versus roll into Default — and is that being tracked, or just the point-in-time delinquency count?
